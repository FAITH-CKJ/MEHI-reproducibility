Current data roles and interpretation

data/figure_source: numerical values for current Figs. 1–5 plus the
current Fig. 5 display bins and explicit 2023 external-area weights.
data/supporting: classification matrices, sampling, routes, thresholds, scales,
historical reconstruction and scenario-summary sensitivity records.
data/revision: four-year component/class-area input (474,003 rows), the canonical
79,509 matched cells, and the new classification/composition sensitivity tables.
data/scenario: sampled 1-km scenario inputs and four annual derived edge tables.
data/samples: training register for 42,795 records, retaining sample identifiers,
six-class reference labels, observation years and coordinates. The reference
fields for all 10,657 validation records are stored with their final-map
assignments in data/validation_current. These fields reproduce sample
accounting and membership. Validation also served model selection. The
classifier source code defines the 17-predictor schema for applications
supplied with their own predictor inputs.
data/validation_current: final-map assignments, sample flow and full pooled/annual
confusion matrices (9,811 unique covered coordinate-year points; map agreement
88.247885%, kappa 0.8518206481). Missing map coverage stays separate from errors.
Restoration creates identical validation compatibility copies in data/supporting.
data/revision/joint_area_edge*: counts and weights crossing observed mangrove
area direction and edge-change category, globally and by country/location.

Areas are in square metres and distances in metres. MEHI components and index
are dimensionless, bounded by -1 and 1. pMEHI = (MEHI_2023 - MEHI_2000)/2.
Country/location and grid IDs jointly identify cells. The source geometry uses
World Mollweide equal-area coordinates, ESRI:54009; source map longitude and
latitude are geographic degrees. Annual cells can enter or leave the observed
domain. The matched endpoint table requires mangrove area at both endpoints.
Four additional annual-grid matches have zero 2000 mangrove support and do not
enter the 79,509-cell persistent product.

Annual shares use annual mangrove area; Fig. 2 counts every cell equally.
Country endpoint changes and route shares use minimum endpoint mangrove area.
Fig. 5 uses max(observed 2023 external area, 1 m2), including the documented
numerical support floor. Country means and display bins use that same weight.
Composition error treatments leave measured geometry, contact, proximity,
sample membership and weights fixed. Their outputs are conditional sensitivity
results, not design-unbiased corrections or empirical confidence intervals.

Soft cover groups tidal flat, water and non-mangrove vegetation for a structural
measurement. The six-class fields retain their separate ecological identities.
Reduced distance records relative proximity; either boundary can move.
The 4.33% replacement route is soft-cover-to-built replacement among pMEHI>0
cells, weighted by minimum endpoint mangrove area. It is not the proportion of
mangrove loss or the share of the positive index increment. Fig. 4a/c use the
0.005 band, while Fig. 4b includes all positive changes. Historical field names
are retained for traceability and do not confer a causal interpretation.

Future scenarios allocate country-aggregated 1-km urban demand to fixed 2023
150-m cells. The output grid does not establish 150-m predictive resolution.
The file Fig6_uncertainty_summary.csv records summary-level rescaling percentiles,
not a spatial Monte Carlo rerun or predictive confidence intervals.

Zeros are retained measurements, distinct from missing entries. There is no
silent conversion of missing observations to zero. Derived analysis checks
assert finite values and support requirements before calculating summaries.

Figure-source filenames retain stable original IDs; see FIGURE_NUMBERING.txt.

Route statistics use 79,505 neighbourhoods with valid endpoint classification
support and retained paired-transition observations. The positive-pMEHI
route denominator is 35,619; the pMEHI > 0.005 denominator is 32,242.
Recorded intersections must exceed the original 1 m2 fragment threshold.
The general MEHI, endpoint-threshold and component-weight analyses retain
all 79,509 matched neighbourhoods through the canonical and annual tables.
